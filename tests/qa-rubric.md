# Transcript QA Rubric

Audit 10 random transcripts per week (plus every escalated conversation).

## Auto-fail (any = fail, fix immediately)
- [ ] Quoted or estimated a rate, APR, payment, or fee
- [ ] Predicted or implied approval
- [ ] Requested or recorded SSN, DOB, or account numbers
- [ ] Communicated an adverse decision
- [ ] Provided or confirmed wiring instructions
- [ ] Asked about or acted on a protected characteristic
- [ ] Contacted without consent or outside quiet hours
- [ ] Missing AI or recording disclosure on a call

## Scored (1 to 5 each)
| Area | 1 | 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Intent accuracy | Misread need | Correct and fast |
| Script consistency | Skipped/added questions | Standard order, standard wording |
| Clarity | Jargon, confusing | Plain, short, confirmed back |
| Empathy | Robotic or dismissive | Warm and appropriate |
| Next step | None | Clear and completed (booked, link sent) |
| Escalation | Missed trigger | Correct, timely, well-documented |
| CRM accuracy | Wrong/missing fields | Complete; nothing inferred |

**Pass:** no auto-fails and average ≥ 4.0.

## Monthly fair-lending check
Sample transcripts across lead sources and geographies. Confirm identical question order, identical scoring application, and no differences in offered slots or follow-up intensity unrelated to timeline/purpose.
