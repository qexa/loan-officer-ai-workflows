# SMS Templates

> Character counts assume average token lengths. Keep final messages under 300 characters.

## Front office
**SMS-01 · Speed-to-lead**
Hi {first_name}, this is {assistant_name} with {lo_name}'s team at {company_name}. Thanks for reaching out about your home loan! Want to grab a quick call? Book here: {booking_link}. Reply STOP to opt out.

**SMS-02 · Inbound recap (not booked)**
Thanks for calling {company_name}, {first_name}! Whenever you're ready to chat with {lo_name}, grab a time here: {booking_link}

**SMS-03 · Booking confirmation**
You're booked with {lo_name} on {appt_date} at {appt_time} ({appt_type}). We'll send a reminder. Need to change it? Reply R. – {company_name}

**SMS-04 · Warm follow-up**
Hi {first_name}, {lo_name} here from {company_name}'s team. Happy to answer any questions about your home loan options. Here's my calendar when it's convenient: {booking_link}

**SMS-05 · Last warm touch**
Hi {first_name}, just checking in one more time. If now's not the right time, no problem. We're here whenever you're ready: {booking_link} – {company_name}

**SMS-06 · 24-hour reminder**
Reminder: your call with {lo_name} is tomorrow at {appt_time}. Reply C to confirm or R to reschedule. – {company_name}

**SMS-07 · 2-hour reminder**
See you soon, {first_name}! Your call with {lo_name} starts at {appt_time}. – {company_name}

**SMS-08 · No-show +10 min**
Hi {first_name}, we missed you for your {appt_time} call with {lo_name}. Everything okay? Reply R and we'll find a better time.

**SMS-09 · No-show +1 day**
No worries about yesterday, {first_name}. Life gets busy! Pick a new time that works for you: {booking_link} – {company_name}

## Application
**SMS-10 · Portal link after consult**
Great talking with you, {first_name}! Here's your secure application link from {lo_name}: {portal_link}. It saves as you go. – {company_name}

**SMS-11 · Resume application**
Hi {first_name}, your application is saved right where you left off: {portal_link}. Stuck on anything? Reply and we'll help. – {company_name}

## Documents
**SMS-12 · Document request**
Hi {first_name}, {lo_name} needs a few items to keep your loan moving: {doc_list}. Upload securely here: {portal_link}. Please don't text documents. – {company_name}

**SMS-13 · Document reminder**
Quick reminder, {first_name}: we still need {doc_list}. Upload securely: {portal_link}. Questions? Reply here. – {company_name}

**SMS-14 · Document received**
Got it, thank you {first_name}! {remaining_count} item(s) left. You're making great progress. – {company_name}

## Closing
**SMS-15 · Closing scheduled**
Your closing is set for {closing_date} with {title_company}. {lo_name} will confirm final details. Exciting! – {company_name}

**SMS-16 · Wire-fraud warning (mandatory)**
Important: {company_name} will NEVER text or email wiring instructions. Before sending funds, call {title_company} at a number you already know to verify. Questions? Call {lo_phone}.

**SMS-17 · What to bring**
Tomorrow's the big day! Bring a valid government photo ID and any funds as instructed and verified by {title_company}. Questions? Call {lo_phone}.

## Post-close
**SMS-18 · Thank you + review**
Congratulations on your new home, {first_name}! It was a privilege to help. If you have a minute, a review means the world: {review_link} – {lo_name}

**SMS-19 · 30-day check-in**
Hi {first_name}! How's the new place? If you know anyone thinking about buying or refinancing, {lo_name} would be honored to help. – {company_name}

## Milestones (W6)
**MS-01 · Application submitted**
Hi {first_name}, we've received your application! Next, {lo_name}'s team will review it and send any document requests through your secure portal. – {company_name}

**MS-02 · Appraisal ordered**
Update: your appraisal has been ordered. The appraiser will coordinate access to the property. – {company_name}

**MS-03 · Appraisal received**
Update: your appraisal is in. {lo_name} will review it and follow up with you. – {company_name}

**MS-04 · Submitted to underwriting**
Your loan has been submitted to underwriting for review. This typically takes a few business days, and we'll update you as soon as we hear. – {company_name}

**MS-05 · Conditional approval**
Good news, {first_name}! Your file has moved forward in underwriting. {lo_name} will reach out about the next items needed. – {company_name}

**MS-06 · Clear to close**
🎉 Great news, {first_name}! Your loan is clear to close. {lo_name} will confirm your closing details shortly. – {company_name}

**MS-07 · Closing Disclosure sent**
Your Closing Disclosure has been sent. Please review it right away and reach out to {lo_name} with any questions. – {company_name}

**MS-08 · Funded**
It's official, {first_name}! Your loan has funded. Congratulations from all of us at {company_name}! 🏡
