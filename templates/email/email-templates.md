## EM-07 · Closing scheduled
**Subject:** Your closing is set for {closing_date} 🎉
**Preview:** Important details and a security reminder

Hi {first_name},

Your closing is scheduled for **{closing_date}** with **{title_company}**.

**Before closing**
- Review your Closing Disclosure as soon as you receive it and send {lo_name} any questions.
- Bring a valid government-issued photo ID.

**⚠️ Protect yourself from wire fraud**
{company_name} will **never** email or text wiring instructions. Before sending any funds, call {title_company} using a phone number you already know is legitimate (not one from an email) to verify instructions.

Congratulations, you're almost home!
{lo_name}
