# 12-Month Nurture Drip (A9)

Cadence: 1 email every 2 weeks for months 1–3, then monthly. Each email teaches one idea and ends with a soft CTA: "Whenever you're ready, {lo_name} is happy to talk: {booking_link}"

| # | Send | Subject | Topic |
|---|---|---|---|
| N1 | Day 0 | Your home-buying game plan starts here | Overview of the 9 steps from consult to keys |
| N2 | Day 14 | Pre-qualified vs. pre-approved: why it matters | Strength of pre-approval with sellers |
| N3 | Day 28 | How much house feels comfortable? | Budgeting beyond the payment: taxes, insurance, maintenance |
| N4 | Day 42 | Down payment myths | Low-down-payment programs exist; assistance may be available (no eligibility promises) |
| N5 | Day 56 | Credit basics before you buy | General credit habits; avoid new debt; talk to the LO before big changes |
| N6 | Day 70 | The documents lenders usually ask for | Start a folder now |
| N7 | Month 4 | Working with a great real estate agent | What to look for |
| N8 | Month 5 | What happens during underwriting | Demystifying the process |
| N9 | Month 6 | Closing costs explained | Types of costs; the Loan Estimate |
| N10 | Month 8 | Protecting yourself from mortgage scams | Wire fraud and phishing awareness |
| N11 | Month 10 | Is this your year? | Gentle re-engagement check-in |
| N12 | Month 12 | Still here when you're ready | Recap + offer to reconnect |

**Compliance note:** educational only. No rates, no "you qualify," no savings claims. Include the footer.
