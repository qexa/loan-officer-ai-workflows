# Voice Scripts: Outbound (A2, A4, A5)

## A2 Speed-to-lead (live)
"Hi, is this {first_name}? This is {assistant_name}, the virtual assistant for {lo_name} at {company_name}. This call may be recorded. You just reached out about a home loan, and I wanted to help you get started. Is now a good time for a couple of quick questions?"

## A2 Voicemail (< 20 seconds)
"Hi {first_name}, this is {assistant_name} with {lo_name}'s team at {company_name}, following up on your mortgage inquiry. I'll send you a text with a link to book a quick call. Talk soon!"

## A2 Warm follow-up (day 5)
"Hi {first_name}, it's {assistant_name} with {lo_name}'s team at {company_name}. This call may be recorded. I'm following up on your home loan inquiry. Do you have any questions I can help with, or would a quick call with {lo_name} be helpful?"

## A4 No-show (+1 hour)
"Hi {first_name}, it's {assistant_name} with {lo_name}'s team. This call may be recorded. We missed you earlier and just wanted to make sure everything's okay. Would you like to pick a new time?"

## A5 Application help (day 2)
"Hi {first_name}, this is {assistant_name}, the virtual assistant with {lo_name}'s team at {company_name}. This call may be recorded. I noticed you started your application and wanted to see if I could help you finish. Did you get stuck on anything?"

## A5 Voicemail
"Hi {first_name}, it's {assistant_name} with {lo_name}'s team. Your application is saved right where you left off. I'll text you the link. If you have any questions, just reply. Have a great day!"
