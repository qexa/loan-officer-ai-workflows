## Setup
Done-for-you installation: load NMLS info, licensed states, products, calendar, branding, and templates; run the 30-scenario test; hand off with the LO onboarding guide.

## Positioning
"Most AI receptionists stop at booking. This one keeps going through documents, milestones, and closing, where loan officers actually lose their time."

## Pricing framework
Price on value (hours saved and loans recovered), not minutes. Typical structure: one-time setup fee + monthly platform fee per LO + usage (voice minutes, SMS) at cost-plus. Validate against your costs and market before publishing.
