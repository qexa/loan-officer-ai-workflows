# Loan Officer Onboarding Guide

Welcome! Your AI team is here to handle admin so you can focus on licensed, high-value conversations.

## What the AI does for you
- Answers your line 24/7 and texts new leads within 60 seconds
- Asks every lead the same fair qualification questions
- Books consults on your calendar and handles reminders and no-shows
- Nudges borrowers who start but don't finish the application
- Chases documents and conditions through your secure portal
- Sends milestone updates to borrowers, agents, and title
- Keeps your CRM updated after every conversation
- Sends you a daily brief at 7:30 AM

## What the AI will never do
- Quote rates, payments, fees, or approval odds
- Collect Social Security numbers
- Communicate a denial or adverse decision
- Send wiring instructions

Those always come to you.

## What you need to do
1. **Keep your calendar accurate.** The AI books straight into it.
2. **Mark consults as Held or No-Show** the same day.
3. **Read the daily brief** and clear urgent tasks first.
4. **Respond to Hot lead alerts** within 15 minutes when you can.
5. **Tell us about recurring questions** so we can add them to the knowledge base.

## Your daily rhythm
| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 7:30 AM | Read the brief; handle escalations |
| Before each consult | Read the pre-consult summary |
| After each consult | Mark held; add a one-line note |
| End of day | Clear tasks; check Hot leads |

## Escalations you'll receive
Rate questions from ready borrowers, complaints, "talk to a person" requests, hardship or distress, closing-week issues, and anything the AI isn't sure about.
