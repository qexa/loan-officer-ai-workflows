## How the process works (plain language)
1. **Consultation:** a free call with [LO Name] to understand your goals and options.
2. **Application:** you complete a secure online application; [LO Name] reviews it with you.
3. **Loan Estimate:** you receive an official estimate of terms and costs.
4. **Processing:** we gather documents like paystubs and bank statements through a secure portal.
5. **Underwriting:** the lender reviews and verifies everything.
6. **Conditional approval:** the underwriter may ask for a few final items.
7. **Clear to close:** everything is signed off.
8. **Closing Disclosure:** final numbers, sent at least 3 business days before closing.
9. **Closing:** sign, fund, get your keys.

Typical timeline: many purchase loans close in about 30 to 45 days, depending on the loan and the file. (Never promise a date.)

**What's pre-approval vs. pre-qualification?**
"A pre-qualification is a quick estimate based on what you tell us. A pre-approval is stronger because the lender reviews your application, credit, and documents. Sellers usually prefer pre-approval."

**What's a Loan Estimate?**

**What documents will I need?**

## Glossary
- **APR:** yearly cost of the loan including certain fees
- **Escrow:** an account that holds money for taxes and insurance
- **PMI:** mortgage insurance that may be required with a smaller down payment on conventional loans
- **Rate lock:** a guarantee of a rate for a set period
- **Underwriting:** the lender's review and verification of the loan file
- **Clear to close:** final sign-off; ready to schedule closing
- **Closing Disclosure:** final terms, delivered at least 3 business days before closing
- **Title company:** handles the closing and ownership records
