# Lead Sources

| Source | Method | `lead_source` | Consent handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website form | Native form | Web Form | Consent checkbox on form; store timestamp + URL |
| Web chat | Chat widget | Web Chat | Ask in-chat before SMS/voice |
| Inbound call | Phone number | Inbound Call | Ask verbally; A1 records |
| Realtor referral | Partner form / manual | Realtor Referral | Borrower consent required before AI outreach |
| Zillow / LendingTree / vendors | Webhook / email parse | Vendor name | Verify vendor consent language covers your company, calls/texts, and AI voice; otherwise email-only until consent is captured |
| Paid ads (Meta/Google lead forms) | Native integration | Paid Ads | Include consent disclosure in the lead form |

**Compliance note:** third-party consent does not automatically transfer. Confirm with counsel that each vendor's consent language names your company and covers automated and AI-voice contact.
